Methods and implementation

Length and robust outliers

core/src/stats/length.js models log-transformed lengths per endpoint and parameter and defines the policy for unseen pairs (ADR-012). core/src/stats/outliers.js provides IQR, MAD and regularized Mahalanobis signals (ADR-013), translating tail values to scores.

Combining and explaining signals

core/src/stats/anomaly.js combines the five continuous signals; the calibrated mode accounts for their different distributions (ADR-015). core/src/detector/detect.js reports the signal and parameter behind a verdict. The score threshold remains outside each signal.
